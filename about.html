About Me!

Hey! I'm StarshinerWing, and welcome to my blog! I'm a college student looking to go into software development with a background in modding videogames. All of my currently public projects are for Slay the Spire, as well as it's sequel, but I'll be sure to branch out soon! I'm a big fan of nature and moths, as you could probably guess by the stickers I put on all my posts, which was a big inspiration for why I've designed my blog the way I have. The initial design phase had me a bit hesitant on if it would actually turn out well, but now that it's become more filled out with blog posts and other links, I've really grown to love this site! I also have some fun hobbies outside of the internet. I play violin, I compete in Yu-Gi-Oh tourneys at my local game shop, I love skiing when the weather cools down. I just love the chance to get out there! If you have any questions about me, about what I do, or just whatever's on your mind, be sure to message me or put it in the question box if I figure out how to add that. I'll see you around!
